Research-based Curriculum Design

Subject: Digital Banking
Module: 1 — Introduction to Digital Banking

Curriculum sampleUniversity learningLearning architecture

Syllabus

  • Meaning of digital banking
  • Digital banking in India
  • Unified Payment Infrastructure (UPI), including BHIM
  • Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS)
  • Mobile money and e-wallets
  • Payment aggregation
  • Demonetization exercise by the Govt. of India and its impact on digitalization
  • Challenges of digitization

Aim

Module one lays the foundation to understand the basics of Digital Banking and its technology trend.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the concept of Digital Banking and changing banking scenario in India.
  • Know the impact of Demonetization on digitization.
  • Understand UPI, BBPS, Mobile Money, and Payment Aggregators.

Content Architecture

  1. Introduction
  2. Meaning of Digital Banking
  3. Traditional Banking vs Digital Banking
  4. Digital Banking in India
  5. Demonetization and its impact on Digitalization
  6. Current scenario
  7. Unified Payment Interface (UPI)
  8. Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS)
  9. Mobile Money
  10. Payment Aggregators
  11. Challenges of Digital Banking
